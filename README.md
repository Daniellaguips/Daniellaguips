### Daniella Guimaraes

In 2021 I started hosting dinners in New York for people tired of dating apps. Fifty-plus events later I turned what I'd learned into [Dani](https://www.velvetties.com), an app that screens every member and gets people off their phones and into the same room.

I build it with a team of AI agents. These two repos are the rules that stop them from breaking each other's work:

- [ai-agent-os](https://github.com/Daniellaguips/ai-agent-os): who takes which task, who reviews whom, and what has to pass before anything goes live
- [agents](https://github.com/Daniellaguips/agents): seven agents that hunt for bugs at the same time, and get sharper every time one slips past them
